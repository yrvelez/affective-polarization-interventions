Partisanship and political interest as moderators of the video effect. 2 conditions: Control, Perception gap video. Primary outcome: post_ap: in-party minus out-party feeling thermometer after treatment, adjusting for pre_ap.. Population: US adults from an online panel, Democrats and Republicans (including leaners), with high interest (very or extremely interested) or low interest (not very or not at all interested). Somewhat interested respondents are screened out.
